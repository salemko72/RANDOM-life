RANDOM life — Delete profile & data

RANDOM life currently uses a device-local profile, not a registered server account.

  1. Open RANDOM life on the device where you used it.
  2. Open Profile in the bottom navigation.
  3. Select Delete profile & data and confirm.

This deletes your name, interests, dietary and allergy preferences, learned choices, favorites, history and completed/viewed items from that installation. Language and sound settings remain. The app returns to its welcome screen. Shared editorial content is unaffected.

If you cannot open the app, clear its storage in Android settings or remove this website’s data in your browser. Browser and installed-app copies are separate. Device backups are controlled through your operating system’s settings. This app currently has no server-side consumer account.

Obriši profil i podatke

  1. Otvorite RANDOM life na uređaju na kojem ste ga koristili.
  2. U donjem meniju otvorite Profil.
  3. Izaberite Obriši profil i podatke i potvrdite.

Brišu se ime, interesi, prehrambene postavke i alergije, naučeni izbori, favoriti, historija i oznake pregledanih/završenih stavki na toj instalaciji. Jezik i zvuk ostaju. Zajednički sadržaj u bazi ostaje sačuvan.

Ako aplikaciju ne možete otvoriti, obrišite njene podatke u Android postavkama ili podatke ove stranice u pregledniku. Svaki uređaj i preglednik imaju zasebnu kopiju; sigurnosnim kopijama upravljate kroz sistemske postavke.

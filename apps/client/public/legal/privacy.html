LifeSpinner — Privacy Policy

Updated: 12 November 2026. Applies to LifeSpinner on Android, iOS and the web. The advertising section applies only to native editions containing Google Mobile Ads; it does not describe the earlier ad-free iOS 0.23.0 build 2.

Audience and scope

LifeSpinner is intended for people aged 16 and over. An intended audience is not an age-verification system. The app does not create a consumer account or require a login. Local recommendation preferences and advertising privacy choices are separate controls.

Advertising data and security

The Android advertising edition uses Google Mobile Ads and UMP. Google automatically collects and shares approximate location inferred from the IP address, app/ad interactions, diagnostic performance information and device identifiers, including the advertising ID and app set ID where available. The purposes are advertising and measurement, analytics, and fraud prevention/security. This is separate from the local recommendation history described below. The SDK transmits this data using TLS encryption. Content requests to LifeSpinner services also use HTTPS.

Ad requests are configured as non-personalized. The app does not offer a global switch that disables all SDK data collection in every region, and privacy options depend on the applicable regional message. You can use Android advertising-ID controls to reset or delete the advertising ID; this does not erase all other identifiers or historical data. See Google's Android SDK data disclosure, Google's Privacy Policy and Cloudflare's Privacy Policy for the respective providers' processing and retention practices.

For a privacy request about information controlled by LifeSpinner, contact the operator below. We can explain local deletion and address information we hold, but cannot promise deletion of Google's independently processed advertising data or automatic deletion of all SDK data within 90 days. Local profile deletion and clearing app storage are not a universal SDK-data deletion request.

Your device profile

You can use the app without personalized recommendations. Your optional name or nickname, interests, dietary restrictions, allergies, intolerances and low-phosphate preference are stored on your device. Favorites, viewed items and history are also stored on that device. Learning from your choices is optional. These profile details are not uploaded to our shared content database.

Shared content and technical requests

The app downloads recommendations and images from Cloudflare-hosted services. Cloudflare processes technical request data, including IP addresses, to deliver and secure the service. The shared D1 database stores editorial content, not consumer profiles. The web edition has no advertising SDK. Native editions with advertising are described below. Opening external links is subject to the destination service’s privacy policy.

Advertising in native editions

Android and iOS editions that contain Google Mobile Ads may display occasional full-screen ads when you return from a result to the wheel. Development and release-test editions use Google test ads. Earlier ad-free editions remain ad-free.

Google Mobile Ads and its User Messaging Platform (UMP) process privacy choices and technical advertising data. Depending on the platform and permitted configuration, this can include IP address and approximate location inferred from it, device or advertising identifiers, ad interactions, and diagnostic/performance data. Google uses this data to deliver and measure ads, secure its services and prevent fraud. Requesting non-personalized ads does not mean no data is collected. See Google advertising privacy information.

LifeSpinner does not send your name, allergies, intolerances, dietary preferences, favorites or local learning history as ad targeting or custom advertising data. It does not request your precise location for ads. Local counters record completed spins, active time and recent ad display times to limit frequency; deleting your content preference profile does not reset these separate ad-frequency limits.

Where required, UMP presents privacy choices before ad requests are allowed. You can reopen available choices through Ad privacy choices in your device preferences. Declining or unavailable consent, network errors and unavailable ads do not prevent use of the catalogue. This edition does not request Apple ATT authorization or intentionally access IDFA for tracking. Any future edition that introduces tracking requiring ATT must obtain the separate Apple permission before that tracking. Google controls retention of data processed by its services under its policies; local profile deletion does not itself delete data held by Google.

Sharing and optional diagnostic reports

Sharing an item sends its title and public item link, plus a LifeSpinner app-information link. Share LifeSpinner sends a short description and the app-information link. The system share sheet lets you choose a recipient or compatible social app; availability depends on installed apps and their supported content types. Your profile name, allergies, dietary answers and account tokens are not included in these share messages. The chosen service processes what you send under its own policy.

Performance diagnostics are kept locally and can be shared only when you choose Share diagnostic log. The report includes app version/build, device/operating-system details and bounded technical timing/error events. This does not automatically upload a report to our server or include the contents of your preference profile. Review what you share and choose its recipient.

Offline catalogue

The app keeps a local catalogue copy and installed reserve content for offline use. Remote images and external streaming, store or website links may still require internet. Clearing the catalogue cache is separate from deleting your profile, favourites and history.

Earlier web experiments

Earlier experimental web previews offered optional Spotify connection. That feature is not part of the current Android/iOS app. Those previews kept Spotify display name, subscription type and access/refresh tokens in that browser tab, separate from the device profile and our D1 database. If you used one, you can revoke its permission in Spotify account Apps settings and clear the old tab/site storage. Requests made by that older player are also subject to Spotify’s privacy policy.

Content Studio

Editorial access is separate from consumer profiles. Studio uses a secure session cookie lasting up to eight hours, stores editorial content and revision records, and records IP addresses for login rate limiting in a rolling fifteen-minute window. Editors can manually send source material to ChatGPT; the app does not automatically send consumer profiles or health preferences to ChatGPT. Poster and book-cover searches are requested by editors through our backend using TMDB and Open Library.

Retention and deletion

Device profile data remains until you delete it, clear browser/app storage or uninstall the app, subject to your device’s backup settings. In Profile, select Delete profile & data and confirm. This removes your stored name, preferences, dietary details, favorites, viewed/completed items and history on that installation. Language and sound settings remain. There is currently no server-side consumer account to delete. Deletion on one device does not delete separate copies on other devices or operating-system backups. See data deletion instructions.

Dietary information

Dietary preferences are optional and used for recommendation filters on your device. Recommendations are not medical advice; suitability depends on your individual needs and verified ingredient information.

Politika privatnosti

Ažurirano: 12. novembar 2026. Odnosi se na LifeSpinner za Android, iOS i web. Dio o oglasima važi samo za native izdanja koja uključuju Google Mobile Ads; raniji iOS 0.23.0 build 2 ostaje izdanje bez oglasa.

Publika i opseg

LifeSpinner je namijenjen osobama od 16 godina naviše. Namjeravana publika nije sistem provjere dobi. Aplikacija ne kreira korisnički račun i ne zahtijeva prijavu. Lokalne preference preporuka i izbori privatnosti oglasa su odvojene kontrole.

Podaci za oglase i sigurnost

Android izdanje s oglasima koristi Google Mobile Ads i UMP. Google automatski prikuplja i dijeli približnu lokaciju izvedenu iz IP adrese, interakcije s aplikacijom/oglasima, dijagnostičke podatke o performansama i identifikatore uređaja, uključujući oglasni ID i app set ID kada su dostupni. Svrhe su isporuka i mjerenje oglasa, analitika te sigurnost i sprečavanje prevara. To je odvojeno od lokalne historije preporuka opisane ispod. SDK prenosi podatke uz TLS enkripciju. Zahtjevi LifeSpinner servisima za sadržaj također koriste HTTPS.

Oglasni zahtjevi podešeni su za nepersonalizovane oglase. Aplikacija nema univerzalni prekidač koji isključuje sve SDK prikupljanje u svakoj regiji; dostupni izbori zavise od regionalne poruke. Android postavke dopuštaju poništavanje ili brisanje oglasnog ID-a, ali time se ne brišu svi drugi identifikatori ili ranije obrađeni podaci. Vidi Google pregled podataka Android SDK-a, Google politiku privatnosti i Cloudflare politiku privatnosti za pravila tih servisa o obradi i čuvanju.

Za zahtjev o podacima kojima upravlja LifeSpinner piši vlasniku navedenom ispod. Možemo objasniti lokalno brisanje i postupiti s podacima koje imamo, ali ne možemo obećati brisanje oglasnih podataka koje Google samostalno obrađuje ili automatsko brisanje svih SDK podataka u roku od 90 dana. Brisanje lokalnog profila i podataka aplikacije nije univerzalni zahtjev za brisanje SDK podataka.

Profil na uređaju

Aplikaciju možete koristiti bez personalizacije. Ime ili nadimak, interesi, alergije, intolerancije i prehrambene postavke, uključujući prehranu s malo fosfora, čuvaju se na vašem uređaju. Tu ostaju i favoriti, historija i oznake pregledanih ili završenih stavki. Učenje iz izbora je opcionalno. Ovi podaci profila ne šalju se u zajedničku bazu sadržaja.

Sadržaj i tehnički podaci

Preporuke i slike preuzimaju se preko Cloudflare servisa. Cloudflare obrađuje tehničke podatke zahtjeva, uključujući IP adresu, radi isporuke i zaštite servisa. D1 sadrži urednički sadržaj. Web izdanje nema oglasni SDK. Native izdanja s oglasima opisana su u nastavku. Za vanjske linkove važe politike odredišnih servisa.

Oglasi u native izdanjima

Android i iOS izdanja koja sadrže Google Mobile Ads mogu prikazati povremeni oglas preko cijelog ekrana pri povratku s rezultata na točak. Razvojna i testna izdanja koriste Google testne oglase. Ranija izdanja bez oglasa ostaju bez oglasa.

Google Mobile Ads i User Messaging Platform (UMP) obrađuju izbore privatnosti i tehničke podatke za oglase. Zavisno od platforme i dopuštene konfiguracije, to može uključivati IP adresu i približnu lokaciju izvedenu iz nje, identifikatore uređaja ili oglasa, interakcije s oglasima te dijagnostičke podatke i performanse. Google ih koristi za isporuku i mjerenje oglasa, sigurnost i sprečavanje prevara. Nepersonalizovani oglasi ne znače da se podaci ne prikupljaju. Vidi Google informacije o privatnosti oglasa.

LifeSpinner ne šalje ime, alergije, intolerancije, prehrambene preference, favorite ili historiju lokalnog učenja za oglasno ciljanje ili kao prilagođene oglasne podatke. Ne traži preciznu lokaciju radi oglasa. Lokalni brojači završenih spinova, aktivnog vremena i nedavnih prikaza ograničavaju učestalost. Brisanje profila preferencija ne poništava te zasebne limite oglasa.

Gdje je potrebno, UMP prikazuje izbore privatnosti prije dopuštenih oglasnih zahtjeva. Dostupne izbore možeš ponovo otvoriti kroz Privatnost oglasa u lokalnim postavkama. Odbijanje, nedostupna saglasnost, greške mreže ili nedostupan oglas ne sprečavaju korištenje kataloga. Ovo izdanje ne traži Apple ATT odobrenje niti namjerno pristupa IDFA identifikatoru radi praćenja. Buduće izdanje koje uvede praćenje za koje je ATT obavezan mora prethodno dobiti zasebno Apple odobrenje. Google određuje čuvanje podataka svojih servisa prema svojim pravilima; brisanje lokalnog profila samo po sebi ne briše podatke koje obrađuje Google.

Dijeljenje i dobrovoljni dijagnostički izvještaji

Dijeljenje stavke šalje njen naslov i javni link, uz link na informacije o LifeSpinner aplikaciji. Podijeli LifeSpinner šalje kratak opis i link aplikacije. U sistemskom meniju biraš primaoca ili kompatibilnu društvenu aplikaciju; dostupnost zavisi od instaliranih aplikacija i vrsta sadržaja koje podržavaju. Ime iz profila, alergije, prehrambeni odgovori i tokeni računa nisu dio tih poruka. Za podatke koje odlučiš poslati važi i politika izabranog servisa.

Dijagnostika performansi čuva se lokalno i dijeli samo kada izabereš Podijeli dijagnostički log. Izvještaj sadrži verziju/build aplikacije, podatke o uređaju/operativnom sistemu i ograničen broj tehničkih događaja s vremenima ili greškama. Time se izvještaj ne šalje automatski na naš server niti se uključuju odgovori iz profila. Pregledaj šta dijeliš i samostalno izaberi primaoca.

Offline katalog

Aplikacija čuva lokalnu kopiju kataloga i ugrađeni pričuvni sadržaj za rad bez mreže. Udaljene slike i vanjski streaming, trgovinski ili drugi web linkovi i dalje mogu zahtijevati internet. Brisanje keša kataloga odvojeno je od brisanja profila, favorita i historije.

Raniji web eksperimenti

Raniji eksperimentalni web pregledi nudili su opcionalno povezivanje sa Spotifyjem. Ta funkcija nije dio trenutne Android/iOS aplikacije. Ti pregledi čuvali su Spotify naziv računa, tip pretplate i pristupne tokene u kartici preglednika, odvojeno od profila uređaja i naše D1 baze. Ako si ih koristio/la, dopuštenje možeš opozvati u Spotify postavkama računa, u odjeljku Apps, i obrisati pohranu stare kartice/stranice. Za zahtjeve starog playera važi i Spotify politika privatnosti.

Urednički Studio

Studio je odvojen od korisničkih profila. Koristi zaštićeni kolačić prijave do osam sati, uredničke zapise i verzije sadržaja te IP adrese za ograničavanje pokušaja prijave u pokretnom periodu od petnaest minuta. Urednici ručno koriste ChatGPT; aplikacija mu ne šalje podatke korisničkih profila. Pretrage plakata i omota urednici pokreću preko našeg servera prema TMDB-u i Open Libraryju.

Čuvanje i brisanje

Podaci ostaju do brisanja profila, podataka preglednika/aplikacije ili deinstalacije, uz moguće sigurnosne kopije uređaja. Otvorite Profil → Obriši profil i podatke i potvrdite. Brišu se ime, preferencije, prehrambeni podaci, favoriti, historija i oznake na toj instalaciji. Jezik i zvuk ostaju. Trenutno ne postoji korisnički račun na serveru. Kopije na drugim uređajima i sigurnosne kopije brišu se zasebno. Uputstvo za brisanje.

Prehrambeni prijedlozi nisu medicinski savjet.

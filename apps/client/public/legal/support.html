LifeSpinner · Privacy

LifeSpinner support

Email cerimovic.ahmed@gmail.com for help with LifeSpinner. Include your platform, app version and a short description of the problem. Do not send passwords, login codes or sensitive health information.

Preferences and data

LifeSpinner uses local device preferences and does not require a consumer account. You can edit your answers in the app. For deletion, see data deletion instructions. In native editions with ads, use Ad privacy choices to reopen the privacy options available in your region.

Problems with an ad

You never need to click an ad to test the app. If an ad causes a problem, tell us which screen you were on and whether reopening the app helped. Sharing a diagnostic log from the app is optional; review its contents first.

LifeSpinner podrška

Za pomoć piši na cerimovic.ahmed@gmail.com. Navedi platformu, verziju aplikacije i kratak opis problema. Ne šalji lozinke, kodove prijave ili osjetljive zdravstvene podatke.

Preference i podaci

LifeSpinner koristi lokalne preference uređaja i ne traži korisnički račun. Odgovore možeš naknadno urediti u aplikaciji. Vidi uputstvo za brisanje podataka. U native izdanjima s oglasima opcija Privatnost oglasa ponovo otvara dostupne regionalne izbore.

Problem s oglasom

Za testiranje aplikacije nije potrebno klikati oglase. Ako oglas stvara problem, opiši na kojem ekranu se to desilo i je li ponovno otvaranje aplikacije pomoglo. Dijagnostički log dijeliš samo ako želiš; prethodno pregledaj šta sadrži.

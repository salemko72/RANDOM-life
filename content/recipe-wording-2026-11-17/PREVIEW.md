# Novi tekstovi recepata — ENG / BHS

Radni paket: nije primijenjen na bazu. Sastojci, količine, porcije, vremena/temperature, nutritivni podaci, izvori i slike ostaju prema postojećem receptu. Samostalno napisani sažeci i kratke upute nisu izjava o konačnoj licenci ili medicinska provjera.

## Smrznuti jogurt s jagodama i čokoladom — NKF

### BHS
Tanki sloj jogurta s jagodama i komadićima čokolade, narezan na male zamrznute zalogaje.

1. Na papir za pečenje rasporedi smjesu jogurta, javorovog sirupa i vanilije. Oblikuj sloj veličine približno 25 × 38 cm.
2. Po površini rasporedi jagode i čokoladne kapljice. Drži u zamrzivaču najmanje 3 sata, a zatim podijeli na 32 komada. Preostale komade čuvaj zamrznute.

### ENG
A frozen yogurt sheet with strawberries and chocolate, cut into small pieces.

1. Stir the maple syrup and vanilla into the yogurt. Spread the mixture on parchment to make a sheet about 25 × 38 cm.
2. Distribute the strawberries and chocolate chips over the surface. Freeze for at least 3 hours, then cut into 32 pieces. Store the remaining pieces in the freezer.

## Brza supa od povrća — NKF

### BHS
Topla supa s povrćem i makaronima u pilećem bujonu.

1. U loncu zagrij bujon zajedno s paradajzom, bosiljkom i lukom u prahu. Kad lagano provrije, ubaci makarone i smrznuto povrće.
2. Kuhaj 6 minuta pa skloni lonac s vatre. Ostavi približno 5 minuta da makaroni dovrše omekšavanje, zatim umiješaj odmjerenu so i biber.
3. Rasporedi u 6 porcija. Za jednu porciju predviđeno je 1½ US šolje supe.

### ENG
Vegetables and macaroni cooked in a warm chicken-broth soup.

1. Heat the broth, tomatoes, basil and onion powder in a saucepan. Once simmering, add the macaroni and frozen vegetables.
2. After 6 minutes of cooking, take the pan off the heat. Let it stand for about 5 minutes so the pasta finishes softening, then stir in the measured salt and pepper.
3. Make 6 servings, using 1½ US cups of soup for each serving.

## Curry od tikve i leblebija — Kidney Care UK

### BHS
Curry s tikvom i leblebijama, poslužen s rižom i chapatijem.

1. Očišćenu i oguljenu tikvu nareži na kocke. Zagrij je na ulju 2–3 minute, pa dodaj luk i curry pastu i nastavi još 3–4 minute.
2. Ulij bujon, poklopi i ostavi da lagano kuha 15–20 minuta. Dodaj ocijeđene i isprane leblebije te paradajz; kuhaj još 3–4 minute.
3. Rižu odvojeno skuhaj u vodi prema uputama s pakovanja, približno 20 minuta.
4. Makni curry s vatre prije dodavanja jogurta i korijandera. Podijeli jelo i rižu na 4 porcije; uz svaku ide jedan chapati.

### ENG
Squash and chickpeas in a curry served with rice and chapati.

1. Dice the peeled, deseeded squash. Cook it in the oil for 2–3 minutes, then add the onion and curry paste for a further 3–4 minutes.
2. Pour in the stock and simmer under a lid for 15–20 minutes. Stir in the drained, rinsed chickpeas and tomatoes and cook for another 3–4 minutes.
3. In a separate pan, cook the rice in water following the packet directions, about 20 minutes.
4. Take the curry off the heat before adding the yogurt and cilantro. Split the curry and rice into 4 servings and serve one chapati with each.

## Salata od kelja i prokulica — DaVita

### BHS
Hrskava salata od kelja i prokulica, s brusnicama i preljevom od meda i senfa.

1. S kelja ukloni tvrde stabljike, a listove sitno nareži i odmjeri 3 US šolje. Naribaj 2 US šolje prokulica i nasjeckaj ljutiku.
2. U zdjeli sjedini kelj, prokulice, ljutiku i brusnice. Za preljev dobro promiješaj ulje, sirće, med, začinsku mješavinu, senf i biber.
3. Prelij salatu, izmiješaj i stavi u frižider na najmanje 30 minuta. Predviđeno je 6 porcija od po pola US šolje.

### ENG
Finely cut kale and Brussels sprouts with cranberries and a honey-mustard dressing.

1. Discard the tough kale stems. Finely cut enough leaves to fill 3 US cups, grate 2 US cups of Brussels sprouts and chop the shallots.
2. Put the prepared greens, shallots and cranberries in a bowl. Whisk the oil, vinegar, honey, seasoning blend, mustard and pepper for the dressing.
3. Toss the salad with the dressing and refrigerate for at least 30 minutes. The stated yield is 6 servings of half a US cup each.

## Tjestenina s pestom od bosiljka i oraha — AKF

### BHS
Linguine s pestom od bosiljka, rukole, oraha i bijelog luka.

1. Skuhaj linguine u vodi prema uputama na pakovanju, do al dente teksture, pa ocijedi.
2. U procesoru usitni bosiljak, rukolu, orahe i bijeli luk. Dok uređaj radi, ulijevaj maslinovo ulje u tankom mlazu; kad se sve poveže, dodaj biber.
3. Pesto rasporedi po tjestenini i podijeli na 4 porcije. Ova procjena važi za navedeni sastav: bez dodatnog sira i bez zamjene oraha drugim orašastim plodovima ili sjemenkama.

### ENG
Linguine tossed with a basil, arugula, walnut and garlic pesto.

1. Boil the linguine in water until al dente, following the packet instructions, and drain.
2. Place the basil, arugula, walnuts and garlic in a processor. Run the machine while slowly pouring in the olive oil; add pepper once the mixture comes together.
3. Coat the pasta with pesto and make 4 servings. Keep the listed composition for this assessment: no added cheese and no replacement of the walnuts with other nuts or seeds.

## Pire od karfiola — Almost Mashed Potatoes

### BHS
Gladak pire od karfiola s krem sirom i bijelim lukom.

1. Oprane komade karfiola stavi u poklopljenu posudu prikladnu za mikrovalnu. Zagrijavaj na najjačem programu 8–10 minuta, dok ne budu sasvim mekani.
2. Ocijedi tečnost i pažljivo usitni karfiol u blenderu. Dodaj krem sir, bijeli luk i biber pa sjedini u gladak pire.
3. Podijeli na 6 porcija od po pola US šolje. Zadrži navedenu količinu krem sira.

### ENG
A smooth cauliflower mash finished with cream cheese and garlic.

1. Put the washed cauliflower pieces in a covered microwave-safe container. Microwave on high for 8–10 minutes, until completely soft.
2. Drain the liquid and carefully blend the cauliflower. Add the cream cheese, garlic and pepper and blend to a smooth consistency.
3. Divide into 6 half-US-cup servings, keeping the cream cheese at the listed amount.

## Lisnato povrće s tofuom i susamom — NKF

### BHS
Tofu i lisnato povrće pripremljeni s curryjem, rižinim sirćetom i susamom.

1. Tofu nareži na kocke i posuši. Na maslinovom ulju ga zapeci 3–5 minuta, zatim prebaci iz tave.
2. U tavi omekšaj luk, dodaj curry i narezano lisnato povrće. Poklopi i zagrijavaj 3–5 minuta.
3. Ulij soja sos i rižino sirće, kratko prokuhaj i vrati tofu. Na kraju dodaj sezamovo ulje i susam. Rasporedi u 4 porcije.

### ENG
Tofu and leafy greens cooked with curry, rice vinegar and sesame.

1. Cut the tofu into cubes and pat them dry. Brown in the olive oil for 3–5 minutes, then lift out of the pan.
2. Soften the onion in the pan, add the curry and shredded greens, then cover and cook for 3–5 minutes.
3. Pour in the soy sauce and rice vinegar, bring briefly to a simmer and return the tofu. Finish with sesame oil and seeds and divide into 4 servings.

## Rižoto s graškom i mentom — Kidney Care UK

### BHS
Rižoto s graškom u dva oblika, svježom mentom, maslacem i sirom.

1. Temeljac drži vrućim. Luk omekšaj na maslinovom ulju, umiješaj Arborio rižu i kuhaj uz postepeno dolijevanje temeljca i miješanje.
2. Odvoji 250 g graška i usitni ga s polovinom mente. Preostalih 90 g graška skuhaj u vodi i ocijedi.
3. Kad je riža kuhana, dodaj pire od graška, maslac, cijela zrna graška, ostatak mente i sir. Umiješaj biber i posluži u 4 porcije.

### ENG
Risotto with both puréed and whole peas, fresh mint, butter and cheese.

1. Keep the stock hot. Soften the onion in olive oil, stir in the Arborio rice and cook while adding the stock gradually and stirring.
2. Set aside 250 g of peas and blend with half the mint. Boil the other 90 g of peas in water and drain.
3. Once the rice is cooked, stir in the pea purée, butter, whole peas, remaining mint and cheese. Add pepper and serve in 4 portions.

## Boranija s lješnjacima i brusnicama — NKF

### BHS
Boranija s tostiranim lješnjacima, brusnicama i koricom limuna.

1. Lješnjake stavi u rernu na 175 °C i tostiraj 10–15 minuta. Ukloni kožicu i grubo ih usitni.
2. Boraniju kuhaj u navedenoj vodi 4 minute, pa je rashladi i temeljito ocijedi.
3. Ljutiku omekšaj na ulju. Dodaj boraniju, brusnice i lješnjake i kratko zagrij zajedno. Dodaj koricu limuna i podijeli na 8 porcija.

### ENG
Green beans with toasted hazelnuts, cranberries and lemon zest.

1. Toast the hazelnuts in a 175°C oven for 10–15 minutes, then rub off the skins and chop.
2. Boil the beans in the listed water for 4 minutes. Cool them and drain thoroughly.
3. Soften the shallots in the oil. Add the beans, cranberries and hazelnuts and heat together briefly. Finish with lemon zest and make 8 servings.

## Male palačinke — NKF Kidney Cooking

### BHS
Male palačinke od jednostavne smjese s jajetom i mlijekom.

1. U zdjeli razmuti jaje s brašnom, šećerom i praškom za pecivo. Dolij mlijeko i vodu i miješaj dok smjesa ne bude glatka.
2. Zagrij navedeno ulje u tavi. Za svaku palačinku ulij četvrtinu US šolje smjese i peci s obje strane, dok ne porumeni i bude pečena u sredini.
3. Jedna mala palačinka čini porciju. Nutritivni podaci ne obuhvataju sirup, dodatni maslac ili druge preljeve.

### ENG
Small pancakes made with an egg-and-milk batter.

1. Mix the beaten egg with the flour, sugar and baking powder. Pour in the milk and water and stir to form a smooth batter.
2. Heat the listed oil in a frying pan. Use a quarter-US-cup measure of batter per pancake and cook on both sides until golden and cooked through.
3. One small pancake is one serving. The nutrient figures do not include syrup, extra butter or other toppings.

## Salata od breskve i krastavca — AKF

### BHS
Svježa salata s breskvom, krastavcem, cherry paradajzom i mentom.

1. Breskvu i krastavac nareži na komade od približno 1 cm. Cherry paradajz prepolovi, a mladi luk i mentu sitno nasjeckaj.
2. Pripremljene sastojke promiješaj sa sokom limete.
3. Mlade listove salate rasporedi na 4 tanjira. Preko njih dodaj smjesu i biber; ne dodaj med ili ulje.

### ENG
A fresh salad of peach, cucumber, cherry tomatoes and mint.

1. Cut the peach and cucumber into pieces about 1 cm across. Halve the cherry tomatoes and finely chop the scallion and mint.
2. Toss the prepared ingredients with the lime juice.
3. Arrange the baby salad leaves on 4 plates and spoon the mixture over them. Add pepper, without adding honey or oil.

## Salata od karfiola i krompira — KNI

### BHS
Rashlađena salata od krompira i karfiola s koprom i veganskom majonezom.

1. Krompir nareži na kockice i kuhaj u vodi 5 minuta. Dodaj cvjetiće karfiola i kuhaj još približno 5 minuta, do mekoće. Ocijedi i ostavi da se ohladi.
2. Za preljev spoji majonezu, sitno narezan kiseli krastavac, sirće, senf, kopar i biber.
3. Preljev pomiješaj s ohlađenim povrćem, lukom i celerom. Stavi u frižider i podijeli na 6 porcija.

### ENG
A chilled potato and cauliflower salad with dill and vegan mayonnaise.

1. Dice the potato and boil in water for 5 minutes. Add the cauliflower florets and cook for about 5 more minutes, until tender. Drain and leave to cool.
2. Make the dressing by mixing the mayonnaise, finely chopped pickle, vinegar, mustard, dill and pepper.
3. Fold the cooled vegetables, onions and celery into the dressing. Refrigerate and divide into 6 servings.

## Smuti od jagode i ananasa — NKF

### BHS
Hladni smuti od jagoda i ananasa s biljnim jogurtom i napitkom od badema.

1. U blender stavi navedeni biljni jogurt, napitak od badema, ananas, jagode i led. Miksaj do ujednačene, glatke teksture.
2. Ulij u 2 porcije od po 8 fl oz. Za postojeću nutritivnu procjenu koristi navedene biljne proizvode i provjeri njihove deklaracije.

### ENG
A chilled strawberry and pineapple smoothie with almond-based yogurt and drink.

1. Put the listed plant yogurt, almond drink, pineapple, strawberries and ice in a blender. Blend to an even, smooth consistency.
2. Pour into 2 servings of 8 fl oz each. Retain the specified plant products for the existing nutrient assessment and check their labels.

## Bolognese od crvene leće — Kidney Care UK

### BHS
Sos od crvene leće i paradajza s povrćem, poslužen uz tjesteninu bez jaja.

1. Luk, mrkvu, bijeli luk i celer sitno nareži i omekšavaj na ulju 10 minuta. Kocku za supu rastopi u navedenoj količini kipuće vode.
2. Povrću dodaj leću, paradajz, bosiljak, koncentrat paradajza, pripremljeni temeljac i balzamično sirće. Ostavi da lagano kuha oko 20 minuta.
3. Tjesteninu skuhaj odvojeno. Sos i tjesteninu rasporedi u 4 porcije, bez dodavanja sira.

### ENG
Red lentils and vegetables in a tomato sauce, served with egg-free pasta.

1. Finely chop the onion, carrot, garlic and celery and soften in the oil for 10 minutes. Dissolve the stock cube in the listed amount of boiling water.
2. Add the lentils, tomatoes, basil, tomato purée, prepared stock and balsamic vinegar to the vegetables. Simmer for about 20 minutes.
3. Cook the pasta in a separate pan. Divide the sauce and pasta into 4 servings without adding cheese.

## Pečene korpice od jaja, svinjetine i paprika — NKF

### BHS
Slane korpice iz kalupa za muffine, s jajima, svinjetinom, lukom i paprikama.

1. Rernu zagrij na 175 °C. Podmaži 8 udubljenja kalupa za muffine.
2. Mljevenu svinjetinu pomiješaj s navedenim začinima i isprži do unutrašnje temperature od najmanje 71 °C. Ocijedi višak masnoće.
3. Razmuti jaja s mlijekom i navedenom soli. Umiješaj meso, luk i paprike, pa smjesu ravnomjerno rasporedi u kalup.
4. Peci 18–22 minute, dok se jaja potpuno ne stisnu. Jedna korpica čini porciju.

### ENG
Savoury muffin-tin cups with eggs, pork, onion and peppers.

1. Preheat the oven to 175°C and grease 8 wells of a muffin tin.
2. Combine the ground pork with the listed seasonings and fry until its internal temperature reaches at least 71°C. Drain off excess fat.
3. Beat the eggs with the milk and listed salt. Stir in the pork, onion and peppers and distribute evenly between the wells.
4. Bake for 18–22 minutes, until the eggs are fully set. Serve one cup per portion.

## Začinjeni hljeb s mrkvom i grožđicama — NKF

### BHS
Hljeb s mrkvom i grožđicama, začinjen cimetom, muškatnim oraščićem i klinčićem.

1. Pripremi nauljen kalup veličine 23 × 13 cm i zagrij rernu na 190 °C. U jednoj posudi pomiješaj suhe sastojke, a u drugoj preostale sastojke.
2. Spoji sadržaj obje posude uz kratko miješanje i prebaci u kalup. Peci 45–60 minuta, dok i sredina ne bude pečena.
3. Sačekaj da se hljeb ohladi, zatim ga nareži na 18 kriški.

### ENG
A carrot and raisin loaf with cinnamon, nutmeg and cloves.

1. Oil a 23 × 13 cm loaf tin and heat the oven to 190°C. Combine the dry ingredients in one bowl and the remaining ingredients in another.
2. Bring the two mixtures together with only brief mixing, then transfer to the tin. Bake for 45–60 minutes, until the centre is cooked through.
3. Let the loaf cool before cutting it into 18 slices.

## Kedgeree s lososom — Kidney Care UK

### BHS
Riža s lososom, graškom i boranijom, uz kuhana jaja i peršun.

1. Luk zagrijavaj na maslinovom ulju oko 10 minuta, dok ne omekša. Rižu za to vrijeme skuhaj u odvojenoj posudi.
2. Grašak i boraniju skuhaj u dosta vode i dobro ocijedi. Dodaj ih luku zajedno s rižom i curryjem.
3. Umiješaj prethodno kuhani losos i sve ravnomjerno zagrij do vrućeg. Podijeli na 4 porcije i dodaj tvrdo kuhana jaja narezana na četvrtine te peršun.

### ENG
Rice with salmon, peas and green beans, served with cooked eggs and parsley.

1. Cook the onion in the olive oil for about 10 minutes, until soft. Meanwhile, cook the rice in a separate pan.
2. Boil the peas and green beans in plenty of water and drain well. Add them to the onion with the rice and curry.
3. Fold in the already cooked salmon and heat the mixture thoroughly. Divide into 4 servings and add the fully hard-boiled eggs, cut into quarters, and the parsley.

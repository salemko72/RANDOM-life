# LifeSpinner

Approved direction on 19 October 2026, superseding LifeShuffler:
- Name: LifeSpinner (capital L and S; two n characters).
- Retain the five-color wheel as the dot on the i in Life, at the final modest
  spacing above its stem. No separate emblem above the wordmark.
- Life light rose-taupe; Spinner softer dark brown. Slightly looser tracking.
- Preserve the reduced splash/home logo widths.
- Visible action terminology is SPINN / Spinn again / Spinn ponovo.
- Launcher icon remains the approved standalone wheel; display name changes.

Current optimized wordmark: `apps/client/src/web/assets/brand/lifespinner-wordmark.webp`,
1400 × 288 with transparency. Native identities, signing and storage keys retain
compatibility with existing installs.

The two LifeShuffler 0.21.0 (4) EAS builds were explicitly canceled on user request:
bca4ec26-886d-4ef5-af2d-829a34736cba and d0eecdae-3311-4633-94e8-1a335893705c.
No replacement APK/AAB is requested by this branding-only change.
